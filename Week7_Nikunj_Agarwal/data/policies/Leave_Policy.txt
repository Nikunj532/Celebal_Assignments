LEAVE POLICY

Purpose:
The purpose of this policy is to provide employees with guidelines regarding leave entitlement and approval procedures.

Annual Leave:
- Employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per calendar year.
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance.

Sick Leave:
- Employees are entitled to 12 sick leave days annually.
- Medical documentation may be required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.

Emergency Leave:
- Emergency leave may be granted based on individual circumstances and management approval.

Leave Approval:
- All leave requests are subject to reporting manager approval.
- Business requirements may affect leave approval decisions.

Policy Compliance:
Failure to comply with leave procedures may result in leave being considered unauthorized.
