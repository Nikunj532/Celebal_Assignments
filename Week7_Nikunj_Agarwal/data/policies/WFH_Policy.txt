WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Purpose:
This policy outlines the guidelines for employees working remotely.

Eligibility:
- Employees may work from home with manager approval.
- Remote work arrangements are subject to job responsibilities and performance requirements.

Working Hours:
- Employees must be available during standard business hours.
- Attendance in scheduled meetings is mandatory.

Communication:
- Employees must maintain regular communication through approved company channels.
- Emails and messages should be responded to within a reasonable timeframe.

Data Security:
- Company devices should be used whenever possible.
- Confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Performance:
- Productivity and work quality expectations remain the same as office-based work.
